§1
================================================
                      Human                      
================================================

Humans are a plain species with no special abilities.
Instead of having unique powers, they have balanced aptitudes with all values at 0.


* They have standard HP and base stats.
* Every 5 levels, one random stat increases by 1.


§2
================================================
                      Witch                      
================================================

A naturally gifted magic user as a species.
In Gensokyo, they are considered youkai.
They have extremely high magical aptitude and can wield any magic with ease, but their physical abilities are weak.

* 10% less HP compared to Humans.
* Starts with -2 Strength and +2 Intelligence compared to Humans.
* Gains +1 Intelligence every 4 levels.



§3
================================================
                     Fairy                       
================================================

A manifestation of natural phenomena in Gensokyo — small beings that dwell in all things.
They have small bodies and wings, and love playing pranks.
They are skilled with elemental magic, but are physically fragile and too small to wear heavy armor.
Though their HP is low, they overcome danger through high regeneration and stealth.


* 30% less HP compared to Humans.
* Starts with -2 Strength, +1 Intelligence, and +1 Dexterity compared to Humans.
* Gains +1 Intelligence or Dexterity every 5 levels.
* Cannot wear heavy armor due to small size.
* Has high HP regeneration.
* Has high MP regeneration.
* Moves stealthily.
* Takes half damage from clouds.



§4
================================================
                   Crow Tengu                  
================================================

Tengu with crow-like wings who live communally in the Youkai Mountain.
Many crow tengu work in journalism, gathering information across the land.
They can fly, possess decent combat ability, and have good aptitude in air magic.
They fight best by focusing on evasion rather than armor.


* Has the same HP as Humans.
* Starts with -1 Intelligence and +1 Dexterity compared to Humans.
* Gains +1 Strength or Dexterity every 5 levels.
* Can fly.



§5
================================================
                   Wolf Tengu                    
================================================

Tengu with wolf-like traits who live communally in the Youkai Mountain.
They mainly serve as patrols and guards, driving away intruders.
They are well-suited for heavy-armored warriors, with high aptitudes in armor and shields.


* Has 10% more HP compared to Humans.
* Starts with +2 Strength, -1 Intelligence, and -1 Dexterity compared to Humans.
* Gains +1 Strength every 4 levels.
* Possesses strong jaws capable of biting enemies.



§6
================================================
                     Kappa                      
================================================

Youkai who dwell together in Genbu Ravine on the mid-slope of the Youkai Mountain.
They are excellent swimmers and often live near water, spending their time crafting machines thanks to their dexterous hands.
They have high evasion, are skilled with activated items, and have a knack for ice magic.


* Has 10% less HP compared to Humans.
* Gains +1 random stat every 5 levels.
* Can swim.



§7
================================================
                    Nekomata                    
================================================

A cat youkai with feline ears and a tail.
Like a true cat, it moves quickly and silently.
Its fast movement makes it good at escaping enemies, and it excels in unarmed combat, though it's not good with weapons.
It also has good aptitude for alchemy and mental magic, and especially excels in space-time magic.


* Has 20% less HP compared to Humans.
* Starts with -2 Strength and +2 Dexterity compared to Humans.
* Gains +1 Dexterity every 5 levels.
* Moves quickly. (Consumes only 80% of a turn to move.)  
* Has sharp claws.
* At level 15, it grows thick fur, gaining Cold Resistance 1 and +3 AC.



§8
================================================
                      Oarfish                  
================================================

A ribbonfish youkai who resides in the Dragon Palace and descends to the surface to predict earthquakes.
They usually swim leisurely among the clouds.
They move slowly compared to other species, but possess high HP and innate resistance to electricity.
They are especially skilled in air (electric) magic.


* Has 20% more HP compared to Humans.
* Gains +1 random stat every 5 levels.
* Moves slowly. (Consumes 120% of a turn to move.)  
* Can swim.
* Starts with Electricity Resistance 1.
* Has tough scales, granting +3 AC.
* At level 9, their scales grow stronger, increasing AC to +6.
* At level 18, their scales toughen further, increasing AC to +9.



§9
================================================
                    Tsukumogami                  
================================================

A youkai born when a soul inhabits an old, long-used object.
They carry the tool that serves as their true body.
Since their body is an object, they start with poison resistance.
Though small in stature, they are skilled with equipment and well-suited for light-armored combat.
Their magic aptitude isn't high, but they have decent aptitude for alchemy.


* Has 10% less HP compared to Humans.
* Gains +1 Strength or Dexterity every 5 levels.
* Too small to wear heavy armor.
* Starts with Poison Resistance.



§10
================================================
                      Oni                      
================================================

One of the most powerful youkai in Gensokyo, known for overwhelming strength and magical energy.
They possess immense HP and large horns, with astonishing combat aptitude.
Due to their heavy drinking nature, they suffer almost no penalties when drunk.
They excel at brute-force combat, using raw physical power to crush foes.
However, their overconfidence in strength makes them reluctant to use tools or magic.


* Has 30% more HP compared to Humans.
* Starts with +3 Strength, -2 Intelligence, and -1 Dexterity compared to Humans.
* Gains +1 Strength every 3 levels.
* Too large to wear heavy armor.
* Starts with large horns.
* Starts with the “Heavy Drinker” trait.



§11
================================================
                   Half-Youkai                   
================================================

A half-blood born of both human and unknown youkai lineage.
Though initially human-like, their youkai blood awakens as they grow.
Depending on the type of youkai they inherit from, they gain different traits.
Before this awakening, they are mostly identical to humans — and even have lower aptitudes.
This is a late-blooming species that gradually becomes a powerful youkai.


* Has the same HP as Humans.
* Gains +1 random stat every 5 levels.
* Youkai blood awakens at levels 4, 9, 13, and 18.
* At level 4, the inherited youkai type is revealed.
* Beast bloods favor melee, Oni blood grants endurance,  
  Dragon blood leans toward elemental powers, and Demon blood grants magical traits.



§12
================================================
                     Ghost                     
================================================

A youkai spirit that died but could not pass on, now wandering the living world.
Though lacking a physical body, it can still touch and speak.
It has no feet, so it cannot wear shoes, but it moves more stealthily than any other.
It also has innate resistance to poison.
While not suited for close combat or heavy equipment,  
it excels at ambush and assassination, and has high aptitude in mind magic.


* Has 10% more HP compared to Humans.
* Gains +1 random stat every 5 levels.
* Moves with extreme stealth.
* Starts with Poison Resistance.
* Has no feet — floats with ghostly legs.



§13
================================================
                    Vampire                   
================================================

A Western youkai from outside Gensokyo — the vampire.
Known for their exceptional strength, magical power, and regeneration, they are among the strongest species.
However, they are also burdened with many weaknesses, such as sunlight, garlic, and holy symbols.
Their MP recovers slowly, but they can cast spells using HP as a substitute.
They are especially skilled in handling familiars, giving them high aptitude in summoning magic.
They also have strong aptitudes in stealth, unarmed combat, and evasion in addition to magic.


* Gains +1 random stat every 7 levels.
* Recovers HP very quickly.
* MP regeneration is slow.
* Power depletes quickly.
* Has sharp vision and can see invisible.
* Can use HP (2x cost) instead of MP to cast spells.

§14
================================================
                     Warrior                     
================================================

A classic heavy-armored fighter who favors close combat.
Starts with no special items, but carries a reliable shield.


* Starts with +2 maximum HP.
* Begins with +3 Strength, -6 Intelligence, and +1 Dexterity.
* Starts with a weapon of choice, chain armor, and an iron shield.
* If unable to wear chain armor, starts with leather armor and a cloak instead.



§15
================================================
                     Magician                     
================================================

A magician adept in various types of magic.
They are not specialized in any one school,  
but can flexibly use a variety of low-level spells to handle different situations.


* Starts with -2 maximum HP and +5 maximum MP.
* Begins with -6 Strength and +6 Intelligence.
* Starts with a robe and a novice magician’s spellbook.
* Starts knowing Magic Danmaku.
* Can learn Blink, Diamond Hardness, Summon Option, Mephitic Cloud, and Conjure Flame.



§16
================================================
                  Shrine Maiden             
================================================

A melee-focused class who serves Kanako and fights using Kanako's divine power.


* Starts with +1 maximum HP and MP.
* Begins with +1 Strength, -2 Intelligence, and +1 Dexterity.
* Starts with a +1 Gohei and a Celestial Shrine Maiden Robe.
* Begins with faith in Kanako.


§17
================================================
                    Gladiator                    
================================================

A hybrid evasion-type warrior who balances both melee and ranged combat.


* Starts with +2 maximum HP.
* Begins with +2 Strength, -4 Intelligence, and +2 Dexterity.
* Starts with a weapon of choice, leather armor, wooden shield, and 8 venom shurikens.



§18
================================================
                      Monk                     
================================================

A fighter with innate talent in unarmed combat.
Does not start with many items, but begins with high unarmed combat skill.


* Starts with +2 maximum HP.
* Starts wearing only a robe.



§19
================================================
                    Spellblade                   
================================================

A spellblade who uses both magic and melee, specializing in self-buff spells.
Begins with magic useful for frontline combat.


* Starts with +1 maximum HP and MP.
* Begins with -6 Strength and +6 Intelligence.
* Starts with a robe and a novice magician’s spellbook.
* Starts knowing Veiling.
* Can learn Diamond Hardness, Poison Body, Silence.



§20
================================================
                    Berserker                     
================================================

Starts with extreme stats and very aggressive equipment.


* Starts with +2 maximum HP.
* Begins with +5 Strength, -7 Intelligence, and +2 Dexterity.
* Starts with a hand axe, a robe, and one potion of strength.



§21
================================================
                   Pyromancer                    
================================================

An elemental magician who starts by learning fire magic.
Fire spells focus on dealing damage and follow solid fundamentals.


* Starts with -1 maximum HP and +3 maximum MP.
* Begins with -5 Strength, +4 Intelligence, and +1 Dexterity.
* Starts with a robe and a novice pyromancer’s spellbook.
* Starts knowing Flame.
* Can learn Flame Danmaku, Conjure Flame, Fireball, and Fire Bolt.


§22
================================================
                    Cryomancer                    
================================================

An elemental magician who starts by learning cold magic.
Cold spells often inflict slowing effects on enemies,  
allowing you to deal damage while keeping your distance.


* Starts with -1 maximum HP and +3 maximum MP.
* Begins with -5 Strength, +4 Intelligence, and +1 Dexterity.
* Starts with a robe and a novice cryomancer’s spellbook.
* Starts knowing Frost.
* Can learn Frost Danmaku, Rapid Freeze, Frost Cloud, and Ice Bolt.


§23
================================================
                    Geomancer                    
================================================

An elemental magician who starts by learning earth magic.
Earth spells deal damage that ignores elemental resistances.


* Starts with -1 maximum HP and +3 maximum MP.
* Begins with -5 Strength, +4 Intelligence, and +1 Dexterity.
