<Pad Key Bindings>
D-Pad  - Move
Left Stick + A - Move (Equivalent to keyboard keys yuhjklbn)
↖↑↗    y k u
←·→ <> h . l
↙↓↘    b j n
A      - Pick up, use stairs, pray at altar, wait 1 turn
                         (Left action takes priority based on situation)
Hold A - Recover for 100 turns
B      - Cancel
Hold B - Inspect map
X      - Quick Menu 1 (default: Magic or Throw)
Hold X - Quick Menu 2 (default: Divine Power or Activated Ability)
Y      - Open right-side menu/item window
Hold Y - View carried items
LB     - Auto combat
RB     - Auto explore
LT/RT  - Zoom out/in
