§c beta patch – ver1.209 (2026-10-02)§d
---------------------------------------------------------------------

§y* Feature Additions§d
 * Added three new early-game monsters.
  * Added the Shoebill.
   * It has the same stats as the Crane, but fits the Touhou setting better.
   * It replaces the Crane as a regular spawn. The Crane remains as an Easter egg, appearing at 10% of the Shoebill's rate.
  * Added the Yamanba.
   * It throws fairly powerful axes from range. It cannot throw them at adjacent targets, but still deals moderate damage in melee.
   * It replaces roughly half of the Samurai parties that would normally appear.
   * It serves as an early-game threat to magicians, similar to the role of centaurs in Dungeon Crawl.
  * Added the Tornado Elemental.
   * It is frail but fast, and fires Homing Danmaku as a counterattack when slain.
   * Homing Danmaku will usually miss at point-blank range, making it mostly harmless to melee fighters.
   * It appears in place of some early-game Mooks.
 * Reworked the Scarlet Basement.
  * With Sakuya's help, the Scarlet Basement is now a single-floor dungeon made up of endless corridors.
  * While wandering its endless corridors, you may find a large room containing a rune, a large cache of items, and Flandre.
  * Flandre's overall stats have been reduced, but she now uses her explosions more intelligently.
  * Flandre now uses a special Spell Card when defeated.
   * These Spell Cards are final danmaku patterns that certain unique monsters activate automatically when defeated.
   * Flandre's Spell Card is the first trial implementation of this system. It probably will not be too difficult.

§y* Gameplay & Interface Improvements§d
 * Increased the base damage of the level 2 spell Homing Danmaku by 1, and made its actual damage clearer in its description.
 * Cirno's magic gifts no longer grant spells already found in starting spellbooks.
 * Halved the confusion duration inflicted on unique monsters by the Sun Spell Card.
 * Reduced the Self-Destruct Doll's HP from 50 to 35 and the noise of its explosion from 32 to 28.
 * Reduced the Spear Doll's maximum attack damage from 36 to 32.
 * Only one Goliath Doll now appears on the final floor of the Doll's House.
 * Enemies defeated by Homing Danmaku, Elemental Harvester, and similar effects now count as direct player kills.
 * The weapon selection screen now highlights weapons suited to the selected race's aptitudes.
 * Mutually exclusive runes are now shown on the same line in the rune overview.

§y* Bug Fixes§d
 * Fixed gamepad users being unable to leave Dungeon Suzunaan.
 * Fixed some stairs being generated inside glass.
 * Fixed several incorrect translations.

§c beta patch – ver1.206 (2026-09-20)§d
---------------------------------------------------------------------


§y* Feature Additions§d
 * Added Sakuya and Cirno as playable characters.
  * Sakuya is a tricky maid who specializes in throwing knives.
  * Cirno is an ice fairy who can create ice items every three levels.
 * The Forest of Magic and the Scarlet Devil Mansion once again have an equal chance of appearing.
 * Added Doll's House, a sub-dungeon accessible from the Forest of Magic.
  * It has three floors, always appears when the Forest of Magic does, and contains the Rune of Dolls.
  * It is the counterpart to the Scarlet Basement, but is currently considerably harder. The Scarlet Basement will be reworked in a future update.
 * Reworked the Maid spells Bullet Retrieval and Time Paradox.
  * Bullet Retrieval is now a level 2 spell that retrieves thrown weapons in a single cast.
  * Time Paradox now summons a duplicate that copies your thrown weapons and spells a limited number of times.
  * Throwing Knives and Javelins now gain +3 accuracy when thrown.
 * Junko's final ability no longer inflicts permanent Purification.
 * Expanded and improved the Dungeon Suzunaan wiki.
 * Dolls war is now level 9 spell

§y* Bug Fixes§d
  * Fixed several bugs involving malformed maps, crashes caused by jump attacks, and Okina's backdoors.
