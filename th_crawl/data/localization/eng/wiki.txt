This page is intended to be searchable in a wiki format in the future.

================================================
Touhou Crawl
================================================
Touhou Crawl is a fan game based on both {Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup} and the {Touhou Project}.

================================================
Touhou Project
================================================
A wonderful game. If you haven't tried it, definitely give it a shot.

================================================
Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup
================================================
A wonderful game. If you haven't tried it, definitely give it a shot.

================================================
Stats (Stat)
================================================
In {Touhou Crawl}, there are three stats: {Strength}, {Dexterity}, and {Intelligence}.
Every 3 levels, you can choose one stat to increase.
Additionally, each race may have different starting stats and growth per level.

================================================
Strength (Str)
================================================
Strength is one of the three {Stats} in {Touhou Crawl}.
It increases base {damage} by Strength / 3. -> {Melee Damage Formula}, {Ranged Damage Formula}
It increases the multiplier of {Smash} attacks with {Mace} weapons.
It also affects the {Equipment Penalty}.

================================================
Dexterity (Dex)
================================================
Dexterity is one of the three {Stats} in {Touhou Crawl}.
It increases base {damage} by Dexterity / 6. -> {Melee Damage Formula}, {Ranged Damage Formula}
{Accuracy} is also increased by Dexterity. -> {Melee Accuracy Formula}, {Ranged Accuracy Formula}
Additionally, Dexterity affects {Stealth}, {Evasion}, and {Shields} formulas.

================================================
Intelligence (Int)
================================================
Intelligence is one of the three {Stats} in {Touhou Crawl}.
Intelligence increases {Spell Power} and {Spell Success Rate}.

================================================
Equipment Penalty
================================================
A debuff that applies when wearing heavy {Armour} and {Shield}.
Each piece of {Armour} or {Shield} has its own penalty value, and the penalties from both are added together.
This penalty is reduced by 1/3 of your {Armour} or {Shield} skill, but it cannot go below a certain minimum.
If the penalty exceeds 2 + {Strength}/6, it counts as Level 2;
if it exceeds 3 + {Strength}/4, it counts as Level 3;
if it exceeds 4 + {Strength}/3, it counts as Level 4.
At penalty Level 1, text appears gray, and {Evasion}, {Stealth}, and {Spell Success Rate} are reduced by the penalty value.
At Level 2, text appears yellow, and {Evasion}, {Stealth}, {Spell Success Rate}, and {Accuracy} are reduced.
At Level 3, text appears orange, and {Evasion}, {Stealth}, {Spell Success Rate}, {Accuracy}, and {Movement Speed} are reduced.
At Level 4, text appears red, and in addition to the above, all {Action Speed} is halved.

================================================
Damage (Attack)
================================================
Damage is the value that reduces an enemy's HP.
By default, damage is calculated as a random value from 0 to your full damage potential.
For player damage, refer to {Melee Damage} and {Ranged Damage}.
Monster damage varies per monster and is a fixed value.
For actual applied damage, refer to {Player Defense Formula} and {Monster Defense Formula}.

================================================
Melee Damage Formula (Melee Damage)
================================================
Base {Damage}: {Strength}/3 + {Dexterity}/6
{Weapon} Max Damage: Weapon Power * (Weapon Enchant/18 + Weapon Skill/20 + Base Damage/6)
{Unarmed} Max Damage: Base Damage + {Unarmed} Skill * 15/20
Afterward, various buffs are applied, and then 0~Damage is calculated against the monster's {Defense}. 
-> {Monster Defense Formula}

================================================
Accuracy
================================================
For player accuracy, refer to {Melee Accuracy Formula} and {Ranged Accuracy Formula}.
Monster accuracy is calculated as Level * 1.5 + 8 -> {Player Evasion Formula}

================================================
Melee Accuracy Formula (Melee Accuracy)
================================================
Base {Accuracy}: 5 + {Dexterity}/3 + {Fighting} Skill/4
{Weapon} Accuracy: Base Accuracy + 1 + (Weapon Accuracy + Weapon Enchant + Weapon Skill/3) * 3 / 4
{Unarmed} Accuracy: Base Accuracy + {Dexterity}/4
Afterward, various buffs are applied and {Evasion} and {Accuracy} are calculated. -> {Monster Evasion Formula}

================================================
Ranged Damage Formula (Danmaku Damage Formula, Ranged Damage, Danmaku Damage)
================================================
Base {Damage}: 1 + {Strength}/3 + {Dexterity}/6
Danmaku Max Damage: 1 + {Danmaku} Damage * (1 + Base Damage/10 + Danmaku Skill/20 + Danmaku Enchant/10)
Afterward, various buffs are applied, and then 0~Damage is calculated against the monster's {Defense}. 
-> {Monster Defense Formula}

================================================
Ranged Accuracy Formula (Ranged Accuracy Formula, Ranged Accuracy, Danmaku Accuracy)
================================================
Base {Accuracy}: 8 + {Dexterity}/3
Ranged Accuracy: {Danmaku} Accuracy + Danmaku Enchant + Danmaku Skill/2
Throwing knives and throwing spears gain an additional 3 accuracy.
Afterward, various buffs are applied and {Evasion} and {Accuracy} are calculated. -> {Monster Evasion Formula}


================================================
Weapons (Weapon, Attack Delay)
================================================
Weapons are equippable items used to attack enemies.
All weapons have a minimum delay and a maximum delay.
When attacking, the delay decreases from the maximum delay by 0.1 turns for every 2 levels in the weapon skill, 
until it reaches the minimum delay.
Some weapons have 3 stages of {Two-Hand Penalty} when wielded together with a {Shield}.
Depending on the penalty stage, attack delay is increased by 0.1 to 0.3 turns.
Weapon types include {Short Blades}, {Long Blades}, {Polearms}, {Maces}, and {Axes}.

================================================
Short Blades (Short Blade)
================================================
Deal less damage than other {Weapons} but have higher accuracy and faster attack speed.
Includes daggers, throwing knives, and short swords.
Deal higher {Stabbing} damage compared to other {Weapons}.

================================================
Stabbing
================================================
A special attack that guarantees a hit and deals high damage against an unprepared enemy.
Stabbing attacks are divided into 3 stages:
Stage 3 occurs when attacking an enemy that is {Asleep}.
Stage 2 occurs when attacking an enemy that cannot see {Invisible}, or is under {Confusion}, {Fear}, or {Paralysis}.
Stage 1 occurs when using a {Short Blades} with the {Autumn} brand, or when attacking an enemy under {Madness}.
Stage 3 always triggers; Stage 2 triggers with a 25% chance; Stage 1 triggers with a 10% chance.
Stage 3 always guarantees maximum damage, while Stages 1 and 2 use the higher of 0~Max Damage or the original damage.
When attempting a Stabbing with a non-dagger weapon, maximum damage increases by 1 + {Stealth} level * 0.1 times.
When using a {Short Blades} for a stabbing, base damage increases by (1 + {Short Blades} level + {Stealth} level),
and Stabbing damage further increases by 1 + {Short Blades} level * 0.1 + {Stealth} level * 0.1 times.

================================================
Long Blades (Long Blade)
================================================
{Weapons} with long blades.
When there are no enemies in the surrounding 8 tiles,
can use {Slash} Away to cut through eligible ranged attacks that can be {Grazed}.
Includes swords, crescent blades, greatswords, and tetsurin.

================================================
Slash (Slash Away)
================================================
The ability to automatically cut through and nullify eligible ranged attacks
that can be {Grazed} while wielding a {Long Blade}.
Requires at least {Long Blade} skill level 8 and no enemies in the surrounding 8 tiles.
You must also not be under {Confusion} or {Paralysis}.
Slash Away chance = 15 + ({Long Blade} skill - 7) * 1.5.
When using {Dual Wield}, the chance is multiplied.

================================================
Axes (Axe)
================================================
{Weapons} with a single blade on one side, specialized for chopping.
When attacking an enemy, a gust effect occurs, 
allowing the attack to hit up to 2 tiles in a straight line, including the tile behind the target.
Includes hand axes, battle axes, and anchors.

================================================
Maces (Mace)
================================================
{Weapons} without blades, with blunt ends specialized for striking enemies.
Can use {Smash}, which causes {Dazed} on each enemy once.
Includes gohei, broomstick, parasol, dowsing rod, and onbashira.

================================================
Smash
================================================
When attacking with a {Mace}, there is a chance to deal heavy damage and inflict {Dazed}.
Smash triggers if the damage is at least 1, with a chance of {Mace} skill level + 25%.
Damage is increased by 100 + {Strength} * 3%.
Also inflicts {Dazed} for ({Mace} level + 2)/2+1 turns.
When Smash occurs, decorative point items are scattered around.
In addition, a {P Item} is generated, 
but in this case, the {P Item} that would drop on kill will not appear.

================================================
Polearms (Polearm)
================================================
{Weapons} with long reach, consisting of a blade fixed to the end of a long shaft.
Have a reach of 2 tiles and can be used via auto-attack or the v key.
Includes spears, javelin, scythe, and trident.

================================================
Unarmed (Unarmed Combat)
================================================
When attacking an enemy without a {Weapon} equipped, you attack unarmed.
When using unarmed, attack delay is fixed at 0.6 turns, and
the {Melee Damage Formula} and {Melee Accuracy Formula} are applied differently.

================================================
Stealth
================================================
Base stealth is determined by {Dexterity} * 3 + Stealth skill level * 15.
Additionally, {Potion of Gliding} increases it by 50, each stealth trait increases it by 50, 
and each {Equipment Penalty} reduces it by 10.
{Maple Armour} increases it by 150, but this bonus is halved if in bright light.
Each turn, if a random value between 0~Stealth is less than or equal to a monster's {Detection} value,
the monsters notice the player and wake up.

================================================
Detection
================================================
A monster's detection value is calculated as 10 + Level * (1.5 + Detection Level).
Detection Level is unique to each monster, typically ranging from 0 to 3.
This value is reduced by 70 if the player is {Invisible}, reduced by 20 just before invisibility wears off,
and reduced by 40 when walking over {Maple Leaves}.
Detection is checked every turn the monster can see the player,
and is based on the player's turns, so it is unaffected by the player's movement or action speed.

================================================
Armour (Body Armour)
================================================
Armour refers to body {Armour} and provides high defense along with penalties.
Armour has four materials - {Cloth}, {Leather}, {Chain}, and {Plate} - which determine AC and penalties,
and eight types - {Outfit}, {Shrine Maiden Robe}, {Winged Garment}, {Kappa Suit}, {Fire-Rat Robe}, 
{Maid Uniform}, {Doll Dress}, {Maple Armour} - which determine resistances.
Armour has {Equipment Penalty} values based on material, 
and the penalty can be reduced by 1 for every 3 levels in Armour skill.
When worn, the armour's {Defense} value is applied to {Defense} calculations.

================================================
Defense (AC)
================================================
Player's displayed AC is calculated as follows:
Base AC comes from the {Armour} and other equipped {Armour} pieces.
Bonus AC comes from armour enchantments and various abilities.
Actual defense is calculated as Base AC * (1 + {Armour} skill level / 22) + Bonus AC.
Monster defense uses each monster's unique defense value.
For actual damage reduction, refer to {Player Defense Formula} and {Monster Defense Formula}.

================================================
Evasion (EV)
================================================
Player's displayed EV is calculated as follows:
Base evasion is 10 by default and increases by 1 for every level in Evasion skill.
Bonus evasion comes from various items.
Size value is 0 for medium races, 2 for large races, and -2 for small races.
Actual evasion is calculated as 3 * Size + Base Evasion * (12 + {Dexterity}) / (32 - Size) + Bonus Evasion.
Monster evasion uses each monster's unique evasion value.
For actual evasion rate, refer to {Player Evasion Formula} and {Monster Evasion Formula}.


================================================
Shield (SH, Guard, Shields)
================================================
There are three types of Shield: {Wooden Shield}, {Iron Shield}, and {Kite Shield}.
Shields have their own {Equipment Penalty} values, 
which can be reduced by 1 for every 3 levels in the Shield skill.
When equipped, the SH value from the shield is applied to SH calculations.
Base SH comes from the shield's inherent value.
Bonus SH comes from the shield's enchantments and various abilities.
Actual SH is calculated as Base SH * (1 + ({Dexterity}/5 + Shield skill level) / 15) + Bonus SH.
{Battle Sense} manifested by {Half-Youkai} multiplies Base SH by 1.2.
For the detailed formula for guard chance, see {Shield Formula}.

================================================
Shield Formula
================================================
Using the actual SH calculated from the {Shield}, the following formula applies:
Final Guard Rate = SH / (Penetration + SH) * 100%
Penetration is 75, and 60 if the attack can be {Grazed}.
Guarding is not possible while {Asleep} or {Paralyzed}.
Some attacks, such as explosions, cannot be guarded.

================================================
Player Defense Formula (Defense Formula, Player Defense)
================================================
Player {Defense} is applied using the following formula:
Damage Reduction: AC 1~15 increases by 0.008 per point, AC 15~30 increases by 0.01 per point, 
and AC 30+ increases by 0.005 per point.
Modified AC: ({Defense} + 1) / 3
Final {Damage}: ((1 - Damage Reduction) * {Damage}) - 0~Modified AC
Some attacks that pierce defense may calculate AC at half value.
-> {Monster Defense Formula}

================================================
Monster Defense Formula (Monster Defense)
================================================
Monster {Defense} is applied using the following formula:
Final {Damage}: {Damage} - 0~AC
Some attacks that pierce defense may calculate AC at half value.
-> {Player Defense Formula}

================================================
Player Evasion Formula (Evasion Formula)
================================================
Player evasion rate is calculated using the following formula:
Modified {Evasion}: EV 1~15 increases by 0.8 per point, EV 15~30 increases by 1 per point,
and EV 30+ increases by 1.5 per point.
Evasion Rate = Modified Evasion / (Modified Evasion + {Accuracy}) * 100%
If Accuracy is higher than Modified Evasion, 
the evasion rate is multiplied by max(0.5, (Modified Evasion) / (Accuracy)).
Maximum evasion rate is 95%.
For monsters, see {Monster Evasion Formula}.

================================================
Monster Evasion Formula
================================================
Monster evasion rate is calculated using the following formula:
Base evasion rate is 45%. If {Accuracy} is higher than {Evasion},
 the rate decreases by 3.5% for each point of difference.
If {Evasion} is higher than {Accuracy}, the rate increases by 3% for each point of difference.
Maximum evasion rate is 90%.
For players, see {Player Evasion Formula}.

================================================
Dazed
================================================
Dazed is a status effect that reduces a monster’s {Accuracy} by 3 and lowers the cast frequency of {Spells} to 90%.
It is mainly caused by the {Smash} ability of {Maces}.

================================================
Spells (Magic)
================================================

Spells can be learned from a {Spellbook} and cast with the z key.
They cannot be cast during {Silence}, {Mad}, or {Confused}, and cost MP equal to their spell level.
You cannot learn a spell above your current maximum spell level.
You have 1 + {Level} + 2 * {Spellcasting} spell slots; memorizing a spell uses slots equal to its level.
See {Spell Power} and {Spell Success Rate} for details.

================================================
Spell Power
================================================

{Spells} use the following power calculation:
Spell Power = (average level of the required schools + {Spellcasting}/2 + A) * {Intelligence}/10 * B
A can include an Intelligence {Potion} (+4) and {Mima}'s Conjuration bonus (+4).
B can include {Enhanced} (2), {Weakened} (0.5), Mima's punishment (0.5),
the {Half-Youkai}'s enhancement magic trait (1.3), and {Miko}'s magic enhancement (1.5).

================================================
Spell Success Rate
================================================

{Spells} use the following success calculation:
Success value = average level of the required schools + {Intelligence}/6 + {Spellcasting}/4
Difficulty = spell difficulty by level + {Equipment Penalty}
Success rate = (success value - difficulty) * (28 + (success value - difficulty - 1) * -1.07) / 2 %
If success value minus difficulty is greater than 14, the success rate is 99%.
The difficulty values for spell levels 1 through 9 are -6, -2, 1, 4, 8, 11, 14, 17, and 21.
The success value also changes with an Intelligence {Potion} (+2), a wizard's {Ring} (1 + number worn),
{Drunkenness} (-2), {Byakuren}'s enhancement magic bonus (+2),
{Mima}'s Conjuration bonus (+2), and {Space-Time Backlash} (reduced by its value).

================================================
Power (P Item, P)
================================================

Power is the third resource after {HP} and {MP}.
It resembles hunger in other roguelikes, but reaching zero does not kill you.
Below 2.00 Power, {Melee Damage} and {Danmaku Damage} gradually fall, reaching half at 0.00.
You cannot cast {Spells} at 1.00 Power or less.
Defeated enemies have a 50% chance to drop a P Item, while Power slowly falls over time.
It normally decreases by 1 every 25-30 turns; {Power Sustain} can double or halve that rate.
{Haste} and {Invisible} each make Power decrease five times faster.
Some {Divine Powers} and most {Evocable Items} consume Power; {Food} and certain {Potions} restore it.

================================================
Confused (Confusion)
================================================

Confusion is a status effect that prevents most actions, including {Spells}, {Scrolls}, and {Evocable Items}.
Some {Divine Powers} and {Potions} remain usable; a {Potion of Curing} normally removes it.
Movement goes in a random direction. A confused player takes 1.2 times melee damage.
Attacking a confused monster has a 25% chance to trigger Stage 2 {Stabbing},
and a confused monster's {Evasion} is halved.
{Confusion Resistance} prevents it, except for certain effects such as divine punishment.

================================================
Poisoned (Poison)
================================================

Poison deals damage over time. Higher poison values increase both its chance to trigger and its damage.
The chance each turn is 1/5 below 15, 1/3 below 30, about 2/3 below 45, and 100% at 45 or above.
Damage per tick is 1 below 50, 1d2 at 50-99, 1d3 at 100-124, and 1d4 at 125 or above.
Monsters multiply that damage by another 1d3.
Poison decreases by 1 each turn and can accumulate up to 150.
With {Poison Resistance} of at least 1, ordinary poison does not apply; vulnerability doubles poison buildup.

================================================
Electrified
================================================

Each turn, Electrified has a 2/5 chance to strike a nearby creature with electricity.
The strike seeks the nearest electrical conductor in range (electric resistance 2 or lower).
It uses the source's {Spell Power}; see that entry for the calculation.
When a monster causes it, its power is the monster's {Level} * 5.
Power is capped at 50, and maximum damage is 9 + power/3 (about 25 at most).
The effect can last up to 40 turns.

================================================
Paralyzed (Paralysis)
================================================

While paralyzed, you cannot act or move.
Your {Evasion} and {Shield} guard chance become zero, so all attacks hit.

================================================
Graze
================================================

Graze completely avoids an otherwise avoidable ranged attack with a 1/5 chance.
The Flow of Air trait increases this chance to 1/2.
It can last up to 100 turns.

================================================
Silence
================================================

Silence prevents sound-based actions, including {Spells} and {Scrolls}.
It has an area of effect that creates a {Silence Field} nearby.
It can last up to 50 turns.

================================================
Sickened
================================================

Sickened prevents natural {HP} regeneration.
It can accumulate up to 200 turns.

================================================
Veiling
================================================

Veiling is a wind shield that immediately reflects damage to a melee attacker, then disappears.
The reflected damage increases with {Spell Power}, up to 20.

================================================
Invisible (Invisibility)
================================================

Invisible is a beneficial status. Enemies without see-invisible cannot spot you,
and their {Detection} value falls by 70.
{Glowing} or {Sunny Weather} negates invisibility.
Power falls five times faster while you are invisible. The effect lasts up to 100 turns.

================================================
Muted
================================================

Muted is a status applied to enemies. While it lasts, they cannot use sound-based actions,
including {Spells} and certain abilities.

================================================
Grabbing (Caught)
================================================

At two or more levels of {Yuugi}'s {Faith}, each melee attack has a 10% chance to grab a target.
Some of Yuugi's {Divine Powers} gain additional effects while you are grabbing it.
Moving ends the grab.

================================================
Ghost Form
================================================

Ghost Form comes from the {Okuri-Chochin}.
It makes you invulnerable to all damage, but prevents nearly every action except movement.
Each turn it consumes Power equal to 12 - power/3 + accumulated uses.
Power here is max({Evocation} skill, {Level}/3 + {Evocation} skill * 2/3),
so higher Evocation reduces the cost. Ghost Form ends when Power reaches zero.

================================================
Fear
================================================

Fear can be inflicted through {Satori}'s first-tier {Faith} ability, {Mind Reading}.
The target flees from its opponent. Mind Reading can sometimes escalate Fear to {Crushed Will}.

================================================
Crushed Will
================================================

Crushed Will can be inflicted through {Satori}'s first-tier {Faith} ability, {Mind Reading}.
Beyond {Fear}, the target permanently loses the will to fight.
You immediately gain half of its experience value.
Killing it gives no further experience, and it disappears after leaving your sight.

================================================
Mind Read
================================================

Mind Read is inflicted through {Satori}'s fourth-tier {Faith} ability.
When the affected enemy attacks you, its {Accuracy} is reduced to one-third.
An affected animal becomes a full ally, but will not follow you to another floor.

================================================
Mad (Madness)
================================================

Madness prevents complex actions such as {Spells} but greatly increases melee damage.
It adds 1-10 to maximum melee and {Danmaku} damage.
{Confusion Resistance} prevents it, and it can last up to 20 turns.
A mad monster attacks the nearest creature without distinguishing friend from foe.
Attacking a mad enemy can trigger Stage 1 {Stabbing}.
Madness attacks have a 50% chance to inflict Madness for 3-8 turns.

================================================
Enhanced
================================================

Enhanced doubles all attack damage and {Spell Power}.

================================================
Weakened
================================================

Weakened halves all attack damage and {Spell Power}.

================================================
Oil
================================================

Taking a fire attack while covered in Oil immediately sets you on {Fire}.
Certain attacks and oil-covered tiles apply Oil.

================================================
Fire (Burning)
================================================

Burning deals 3-4 fire damage each turn.
Being covered in {Oil} can cause fire attacks to set you on Fire.

================================================
Side Effect
================================================

This status comes from {Eirin}'s fifth-tier {Faith} ability.
HP restored by Eirin's healing ability accumulates as its value.
During the final 10 turns, it deals min(remaining value, remaining value/remaining time + 1) damage each turn.
This damage cannot kill you, but continues until the value reaches zero.

================================================
Stasis
================================================

Stasis is inflicted by enemies. It prevents you from using or receiving the effects
of teleportation {Spells} and items.

================================================
Slay (Combat Power)
================================================

The Slay value increases your maximum melee and {Danmaku} damage and {Accuracy} by the same amount.
A negative value reduces them instead.

================================================
Drowsy
================================================

Drowsy is inflicted by enemies. At 100, it forces you to {Sleep} for about 10 turns.

================================================
Fog
================================================

Fog is a {Weather} effect that reduces everyone's sight to two tiles.
Fairies of the Misty Lake are immune to this weather.

================================================
Thunder
================================================

Thunder is a {Weather} effect. Lightning strikes at random each turn.
It has a 1/10 chance to home in on a nearby enemy; otherwise, it has a 1/2 chance
to strike a random location within eight tiles.
The strike causes an electrical explosion dealing up to one-third of maximum HP.

================================================
Sunny Weather
================================================

Sunny Weather is a {Weather} effect. Everyone becomes {Glowing}, reducing {Evasion}
and negating {Invisible}.

================================================
Tempest
================================================

Tempest is a {Weather} effect that multiplies everyone's movement delay by 0.7,
increasing {Movement Speed}.

================================================
Drowning
================================================

If you cannot swim or {flight} while submerged, you lose one-tenth of maximum {HP} each turn.
Lava also deals fire damage equal to one-third of maximum HP, in addition to drowning damage.
Movement delay rises sharply while drowning.

================================================
Stat Penalty
================================================

This occurs when one of your {Stats} reaches zero. All action delays are doubled.

================================================
Perfect Graze
================================================

Perfect Graze activates when the Amulet of Graze is fully charged.
While active, it avoids all avoidable attacks with 100% certainty.

================================================
Space-Time Backlash (Space-Time Penalty)
================================================

This debuff is the recoil from {Space-Time} magic. Some powerful spells add to its value.
Later Space-Time {Spells} lose an equal amount from their {Spell Success Rate} value.

================================================
Drunk (Drunkenness)
================================================

Drunkenness can be caused by some {Potions}.
Each time you move, there is about a 1/11 (9%) chance of moving in a random direction.
It also reduces your {Spell Success Rate} value by 2.

================================================
Ghost
================================================

Ghost is a passive unlocked at the first tier of {Yuyuko}'s {Faith}.
Every three turns, it checks whether to summon a ghost, with a chance of 5 + {Faith}/8 percent.
Yuyuko's third-tier power raises the chance to 85% for a while.
Summoned ghosts are one level below your {Level} and last 20-30 turns.

================================================
Dimensional Lock
================================================

{Yukari} and some enemies can impose Dimensional Lock.
The visible area is fixed, and you cannot leave its barrier while the effect lasts.
With Yukari's version, you can cross an edge and re-enter from the opposite side.
Enemy-imposed Dimensional Lock does not allow this and also prevents use of stairs.

================================================
Time Stop
================================================

Time Stop is a buff from certain {Spells} and other effects.
Everyone except you stops moving. It lasts up to 20 turns.

================================================
Silence Field
================================================

In a Silence Field, you cannot speak or use {Spells}, {Scrolls}, or certain {Divine Powers}.
Even creatures able to cast without speaking cannot cast inside it.

================================================
Murderous Intent (Murderous)
================================================

Murderous Intent is a buff gained at the fourth tier of {Junko}'s {Faith}.
It makes all your {Action Speed} 1.5 times faster, but ends when no enemy is in sight.

================================================
Haste
================================================

Haste makes all your {Action Speed} 1.5 times faster and can last up to 100 turns.
It cancels out {Slowed} while both are active.
Power decreases five times faster during Haste.

================================================
Slowed (Slow)
================================================

Slowed reduces all your {Action Speed} to 0.7 times normal and can last up to 100 turns.
It cancels out {Haste} while both are active.

================================================
Kyou
================================================

Kyou is a curse corresponding to 1-3 points of bad luck, inflicted by some enemies.
Bad luck can accumulate to 10: 1-3 is Kyou, 4-6 is {Daikyou}, and 7 or more is {BuddhaDeath}.
Melee and {Danmaku} {Accuracy} falls by 5.
Each time you gain a large amount of experience, bad luck decreases by 1.

================================================
Daikyou
================================================

Daikyou corresponds to 4-6 points of bad luck.
At 1-3 the curse is {Kyou}, and at 7 or more it is {BuddhaDeath}.
Melee and {Danmaku} {Accuracy} falls by 7, and about 5% of incoming attacks deal maximum damage.
At this level or worse, a generated floor item has a 1/12 chance of becoming an unfavorable item.
Gaining a large amount of experience reduces bad luck by 1.

================================================
BuddhaDeath
================================================

BuddhaDeath is the worst curse, corresponding to 7-10 points of bad luck.
At 1-3 the curse is {Kyou}, and at 4-6 it is {Daikyou}.
Melee and {Danmaku} {Accuracy} falls by 9, and about 10% of incoming attacks deal maximum damage.
The chance of an unfavorable item rises to 1/6. Whenever you gain experience,
there is a 50% chance that two or three UFOs will land nearby.
Gaining a large amount of experience reduces bad luck by 1.

================================================
Frozen
================================================

Some cold attacks inflict Frozen.
It increases movement delay by a fraction of (Frozen value + 1)/20.
With cold resistance, Frozen is ignored if a 1d5 roll is lower than twice that resistance.
Additional duration from stacking is halved, and the value is capped at 25.

================================================
Flight (Flight)
================================================

Flight lets you fly over water, lava, and certain other terrain.
It lasts up to 100 turns.

================================================
Glowing
================================================

Attackers gain +5 {Accuracy} against a Glowing target.
Glowing negates {Invisible} and halves {Stealth}.
It can build up to 100, and {Sunny Weather} or a halo area can also apply it.

================================================
Swift
================================================

Swift improves {Movement Speed} by reducing movement delay by 2.

================================================
Superman
================================================

Superman is a buff from the fifth tier of {Byakuren}'s {Faith}.
It fixes movement delay at 3 and can last up to 50 turns.

================================================
Night Blindness
================================================

Night Blindness reduces your vision to a single tile.
Monsters can still see you normally. See-invisible makes you immune to it.

================================================
Sleep (Asleep)
================================================

Monsters may be asleep when generated or put to sleep by certain abilities.
An asleep monster can wake through {Detection} if the player is in its sight.
Attacking a sleeping target always triggers Stage 3 {Stabbing}, guaranteeing maximum damage.
A sleeping player cannot act and has no {Evasion} or {Shield} guard chance.
Each turn has a 50% chance to reduce the remaining sleep time by one, and an attack wakes the player immediately.
{Drowsy} can cause Sleep.

================================================
Tracking
================================================

Some enemies inflict Tracking. All enemies on the floor learn your exact position and pursue you.

================================================
Overheat
================================================

Some powerful {Spells} cause Overheat.
If you already have Overheat, gaining more deals damage equal to its current value.

================================================
Gluttony
================================================

Each point of Gluttony raises maximum melee and {Danmaku} damage and {Accuracy}.
It stacks up to 9 and fades if you stop fighting.

================================================
HP (Health)
================================================

HP is your basic life resource. You die when it reaches zero.
Maximum HP is approximately (8 + (1 + 3 * {Fighting})/2 + 9 * {Level}/2
+ {Fighting} * {Level}/14) * (HP attribute/10).
It rises with Fighting and Level; racial and trait modifiers are then applied.
Natural regeneration per 100 turns is approximately (maximum HP + 7) * 100 / (240-300).
Each regeneration trait adds 40 to that rate, while reduced regeneration multiplies it by 0.7.

================================================
MP (Mana)
================================================

MP is spent on {Spells} and certain {Divine Powers} and {Evocable Items}.
Spells cost MP equal to their level and cannot be cast without enough MP.
Maximum MP rises by 1 each {Level}, with another 1 at every third level;
it is approximately Level + Level/3.
Natural regeneration per 100 turns is approximately (maximum MP/4 + 7) * 100 / (80-120).

================================================
Movement Speed (Movement Delay)
================================================

Movement delay is the time needed to move one tile, normally 10.
{Swift} reduces it by 2, Sluggishness increases it by 6, and {Superman} fixes it at 3.
{Frozen} increases it by a fraction of (Frozen value + 1)/20, while {Drowning} adds 10.

================================================
Potions (Potion)
================================================

Potions are consumed by drinking. Unidentified potions appear only by color
until you drink or identify them.

================================================
Scrolls (Scroll)
================================================

Scrolls are consumed by reading. Unidentified scrolls have random names.
They cannot be read while {Silence}, {Silence Field}, or {Confused}.

================================================
Evocable Items (Evocable Item)
================================================

Evocable Items are reusable tools activated while worn or held.
Most consume {Power}, and they cannot be used while {Confused}.

================================================
Spellbook (Spellbooks)
================================================

A Spellbook lets you learn {Spells}, which you can then cast at any time.
You cannot learn spells above your current maximum level.
Memorizing one consumes spell slots equal to its level; you have
1 + {Level} + 2 * {Spellcasting} slots in total.

================================================
Ring (Rings)
================================================

Rings are wearable accessories. You can wear one on each hand.

================================================
Food
================================================

Food is consumed to restore {Power}.

================================================
Spell Card (Spell Cards)
================================================

A Spell Card is an item with a powerful effect and a limited number of uses.
Its power increases with your {Evocation} level.
Identifying it reveals the exact number of uses.
Effects such as a recharge {Scroll} can replenish its uses.

================================================
Danmaku (Ranged)
================================================

Danmaku items are ranged weapons that are thrown or fired.
Their damage and accuracy follow the {Ranged Damage Formula} and {Ranged Accuracy Formula}.

================================================
Divine Powers (Divine Power)
================================================

Divine Powers are special abilities granted by the god you worship.
They unlock as your {Faith} increases.

================================================
Armour Pieces (Armour Piece)
================================================

Armour Pieces are worn to gain {Defense} or {Shield}.
Slots include body {Armour}, {Shield}, headwear, and cloaks.
Heavy equipment causes an {Equipment Penalty}.

================================================
Spellcasting
================================================

Spellcasting is a core magic skill.
It increases spell slots to 1 + {Level} + 2 * Spellcasting,
adds Spellcasting/2 to {Spell Power}, and Spellcasting/4 to the success value in {Spell Success Rate}.

================================================
Fighting
================================================

Fighting is the basic melee skill.
It adds Fighting/4 to base accuracy in the {Melee Accuracy Formula}
and contributes to maximum {HP} through (1 + 3 * Fighting)/2 + Fighting * {Level}/14.

================================================
Dual Wield
================================================

Dual Wield means holding a weapon in each hand instead of a {Shield}.
When using {Slash} with {Long Blades}, the success chances are multiplied.

================================================
Two-Hand Penalty (Two-Handed Penalty)
================================================

Some {Weapons} incur a penalty when used with a {Shield}.
Depending on its stage, attack delay increases by 0.1-0.3 turns.

================================================
Power Sustain
================================================

Power Sustain affects how quickly {Power} falls, doubling or halving its rate.

================================================
Level
================================================

Level is your character level, gained by earning experience.
Every three levels, you can raise one of your {Stats}.
Maximum {HP} and the highest level of {Spells} you can learn also rise with Level.

================================================
Resistance
================================================

Resistance values include fire, cold, electricity, {Poison Resistance}, and {Confusion Resistance}.
Fire, cold, and electrical resistance multiply the final damage taken.
For monsters, vulnerability gives 1.5 times damage, slight resistance 0.5,
strong resistance 0.2, and complete immunity zero.
For players, vulnerability gives 1.5 times damage, one level of resistance 0.5,
two levels 0.25, and three levels 0.125.

================================================
Poison Resistance
================================================

Poison Resistance prevents ordinary {Poison}.
Strong poison still applies at one-third strength; poison vulnerability doubles buildup.

================================================
Confusion Resistance
================================================

Confusion Resistance prevents ordinary {Confusion} and {Madness}.

================================================
Strength Increase (Strength Up)
================================================

Strength Increase is a buff on the player.
It raises {Strength} by 5 and adds 1d10 damage. This bonus does not stack with {Madness}.
It can be obtained from effects such as a Potion of Strength.

================================================
Dexterity Increase (Dexterity Up)
================================================

Dexterity Increase is a buff on the player.
It raises {Dexterity} and {Evasion} by 5 and {Stealth} by 50.
It can be obtained from effects such as a Potion of Gliding.

================================================
Intelligence Increase (Intelligence Up)
================================================

Intelligence Increase is a buff on the player.
It raises {Intelligence} by 5, {Spell Success Rate} by 2, and {Spell Power} by 4.
It can be obtained from effects such as a Potion of Intelligence.

================================================
Trait
================================================

Traits are beneficial or harmful qualities inherent to a character.
They may come from a race or a {Half-Youkai} mutation, or be gained during play through effects
such as {Body Modification}.
Traits can alter stats, resistances, movement, combat, and more.

================================================
God (Deity, Divine Spirit)
================================================

A god grants special powers to followers.
To worship one, choose an appropriate class or find an altar and {Pray}.
Each god grants {Faith} for different actions.
(Some of them are not worshipped as gods in Touhou canon; please accept this as a gameplay
convention.)
The gods currently documented are:
-> {Kanako}, {Yuyuko}, {Mima}, {Eirin}, {Satori}, {Yuugi}, {Junko}, {Byakuren},
{Minoriko}, {Shinki}, {Shizuha}, {Hina}, {Yukari}, {Okina}, {Suwako}, {Joon & Shion},
{Lily}, {Seija}, {Miko}, {Keiki}, {Tenshi}

================================================
Faith (Piety)
================================================

Faith represents the strength of your faith and how much your god trusts you.
It is normally displayed as stars:
*..... : 30
**.... : 50
***... : 75
****.. : 100
*****. : 120
****** : 160
How Faith rises, falls, or is spent varies by {God}; some gods do not use it at all.

================================================
Gift (Divine Gift)
================================================

Some {God}s give gifts to their followers.
Some are granted repeatedly above a certain {Faith}, while others are given only once upon
reaching a threshold.
For recurring gifts, gaining faith reduces a hidden gift counter by 1. A gift is granted
whenever it reaches 0.

================================================
Punishment
================================================

Punishment is incurred by betraying a {God} or performing an action that god forbids.
Each god has a separate punishment count. After enough experience is gained, the count falls by
1 and that god delivers a punishment. This repeats until the count reaches 0.
While under punishment, that god's {Divine Powers}, passives, and initiation are unavailable.

================================================
Yuyuko (Yuyuko Saigyouji)
================================================

Yuyuko Saigyouji is a {God} who governs death and fights through summoned ghosts.
When you or an ally kills an enemy, weak enemies have a 33% chance and strong enemies (usually
{Named} enemies) a 100% chance to grant 1 {Faith}.
Every 3 turns, a ghost-summoning check occurs. Its base chance is (5 + Faith/8)%, and summoned
ghosts last 20-30 turns at one level below you.
Faith also falls by 1 every 1,000 turns. Abandoning Yuyuko gives 30 {Punishment}.
-> {Call Ghosts}, {Hasten Ghost Summoning}, {Binding Soul}, {Yuyuko Punishment}

================================================
Call Ghosts
================================================

Call Ghosts is available at 2 stars of {Yuyuko} {Faith}.
It costs 2 {MP} and 10-15 {Power}, and moves nearby allied ghosts to a chosen location.

================================================
Hasten Ghost Summoning
================================================

Hasten Ghost Summoning is available at 3 stars of {Yuyuko} {Faith}.
It costs 3 {MP} and 3 faith and raises the ghost-summoning chance to 85% for a while.

================================================
Binding Soul
================================================

Binding Soul is available at 5 stars of {Yuyuko} {Faith}.
It costs 5 {MP} and 4 faith and marks one enemy. If it dies, it returns as a semi-permanent
allied {Ghost}.
The ghost gains one rank of cold resistance and poison resistance, and its level is reduced by
2.
Only one bound soul may exist at a time.

================================================
Yuyuko Punishment
================================================

Under {Yuyuko} {Punishment}, hostile ghosts appear around you.
Between (2 + {Level}/10) and (5 + Level/3) are summoned for 20-40 turns, each one level below
you.

================================================
Mima
================================================

Mima is a destruction-magic {God}. Only your own kills can grant {Faith}: 25% for weak enemies
and 100% for strong enemies.
Maximum {MP} rises with faith. At 1 star, kills may restore MP based on enemy strength; at 2
stars, destruction {Spell Power} gains +4; at 4 stars, destruction
{Spell Success Rate} gains +2.
Abandoning Mima gives 30 {Punishment}.
-> {Mima Gift}, {Mima Punishment}

================================================
Mima Gift
================================================

At 3 stars of {Faith}, {Mima} gives a Book of Destruction.
At 6 stars, she gives either Mima's Grimoire or Mima's Sealed Grimoire.

================================================
Mima Punishment
================================================

Under {Mima} {Punishment}, a random magical explosion erupts on your tile.
Its base maximum damage is 15 + {Level}x5. Fire, electric, and cold explosions deal 100%
damage; earth and poison deal 80%; destruction deals 60% and ignores half of {Defense}.

================================================
Eirin (Eirin Yagokoro)
================================================

Eirin Yagokoro is a pharmaceutical {God} whose powers focus on {Potions}.
Praying over a beneficial potion grants {Faith} and changes it into a harmful potion; drinking
potions and, occasionally, killing enemies can also grant faith.
Abandoning Eirin gives 30 {Punishment}.
-> {Eirin Potion Effects}, {Throw Potion}, {Body Modification}, {Emergency Transfusion},
{Eirin Gift}, {Eirin Punishment}

================================================
Eirin Potion Effects
================================================

{Eirin} strengthens all {Potion} effects: healing is increased; Strength Increase, Haste, Slow,
and Invisible last 30 turns longer; and Power potions grant 100 extra Power.
Harmful potions are identified automatically and gain extra effects by {Faith}:
1 star, Poison grants faster movement; 2 stars, Stat Loss restores 15-50 {HP}; 3 stars,
Confusion restores 15-30 {MP}; 4 stars, Slow grants invisibility; 5 stars, Paralysis lasts only
1-2 turns and allows a Soul Shot.

================================================
Throw Potion
================================================

Throw Potion is available at 2 stars of {Eirin} {Faith}.
It costs 20-30 {Power} and one potion, creating a 3x3 cloud at the target.
The cloud is poison, confusion, slow, cold, fire, or harmless mist and lasts from (7 + Faith/8)
to (20 + Faith/8) turns.

================================================
Body Modification
================================================

Body Modification is available at 3 stars of {Eirin} {Faith} and costs 20 faith.
It replaces any previous modification with one random beneficial and one random harmful
{Trait}, and temporarily lowers each of {Strength}, {Dexterity}, and {Intelligence} by 1-3.
Good traits: {Massive Wings}, {Sturdy}, {Potion Affinity}, {Sharp Mind}, {Toxic Body}
Bad traits: {Shrunken Frame}, {Twisted Horns}, {Power Loss}, {Greeting Syndrome},
{Potion Addiction}

================================================
Massive Wings
================================================

Massive Wings are a beneficial {Body Modification} trait.
You fly permanently and move 20% faster, but cannot equip cloaks.

================================================
Sturdy
================================================

Sturdy is a beneficial {Body Modification} trait.
Your {HP} aptitude rises from 10 to 11, increasing maximum HP gained per level by about 10%.

================================================
Potion Affinity
================================================

Potion Affinity is a beneficial {Body Modification} trait.
All effects from drinking {Potions} are doubled.

================================================
Sharp Mind
================================================

Sharp Mind is a beneficial {Body Modification} trait.
It grants +5 {Intelligence} and one rank of confusion resistance.

================================================
Toxic Body
================================================

Toxic Body is a beneficial {Body Modification} trait.
It grants one rank of poison resistance. When an enemy within 2 tiles attacks you, there is a
50% chance to create a poison cloud on it for 4 + 1d3 turns.

================================================
Shrunken Frame
================================================

Shrunken Frame is a harmful {Body Modification} trait.
The {AC} of all body armour except shields is halved.

================================================
Twisted Horns
================================================

Twisted Horns are a harmful {Body Modification} trait.
You cannot equip headgear.

================================================
Power Loss
================================================

Power Loss is a harmful {Body Modification} trait.
Maximum {Power} is reduced by 100.

================================================
Greeting Syndrome
================================================

Greeting Syndrome is a harmful {Body Modification} trait.
When you first see an enemy, there is a 25% chance to make {Noise} 12.

================================================
Potion Addiction (Withdrawal)
================================================

Potion Addiction is a harmful {Body Modification} trait.
After 800, 1,300, and 1,900 turns without drinking a potion, withdrawal rises to levels 1, 2,
and 3. Each level lowers {Strength}, {Dexterity}, and {Intelligence}.
Drinking any {Potion} resets the timer and withdrawal level.

================================================
Emergency Transfusion
================================================

Emergency Transfusion is available at 5 stars of {Eirin} {Faith}.
It costs 100 {Power} and 10 faith, immediately restoring 70-80% of maximum {HP}.
The same amount returns as {Side Effect} damage over about 10 turns.

================================================
Eirin Gift
================================================

At 3 stars of {Faith}, every 15 gift-count reductions cause {Eirin} to give a potion.

================================================
Eirin Punishment
================================================

Under {Eirin} {Punishment}, there is a 66% chance to force a random harmful potion effect.
Otherwise, an experiment inflicts {Side Effect} equal to 70-80% of maximum {HP} and lowers a
random stat by 1-4 three times.

================================================
Satori (Satori Komeiji)
================================================

Satori Komeiji is a mind-reading {God}. Every 100 new tiles explored has a 40% chance to grant
1 {Faith}; kills advance her gift counter by 1.
At 0 stars you can detect invisible creatures, at 2 stars first meeting an enemy maps a random
part of the floor, and at 3 stars you sense nearby enemies.
Satori gives no {Punishment} when abandoned.
-> {Trauma}, {Mind Reading}, {Satori Gift}

================================================
Trauma
================================================

Trauma is available at 1 star of {Satori} {Faith} and costs 3 faith.
It frightens a target for 25-40 turns. A target analyzed by {Mind Reading}, or rarely an
unanalyzed target, permanently loses morale as well.

================================================
Mind Reading
================================================

Mind Reading is available at 4 stars of {Satori} {Faith}.
You channel while reading an enemy's mind; stronger enemies take longer, and targets requiring
roughly over 20 turns cannot be analyzed.
Confusion, paralysis, sleep, or losing sight interrupts the attempt. An analyzed target is
guaranteed to suffer the permanent effect of {Trauma}.

================================================
Satori Gift
================================================

At 5 stars of {Faith}, every 10 gift-count reductions cause {Satori} to give food and restore 5
+ 1d25 {HP} and 9 + 1d15 {MP}.
Independently, it has a 50% chance to cure paralysis, confusion, poison, and disease; a 33%
chance to grant 50 {Power}; a 20% chance to restore drained stats; and a 10% chance each to
grant Strength, Dexterity, or Intelligence Increase for 50-80 turns.

================================================
Yuugi (Yuugi Hoshiguma)
================================================

Yuugi Hoshiguma is an oni {God}. Your own kills grant 1 {Faith} at 33% for weak enemies and
100% for strong enemies.
Drinking sake grants +10 {Evasion}; while {Drunk}, {HP} regeneration gains +100. At 2 stars,
melee attacks have a 10% chance to leave the target {Caught}.
Abandoning Yuugi gives 30 {Punishment}.
-> {Drink Sake}, {German Suplex}, {Throw}, {Howl of Annihilation}, {Triple Ultimate Technique},
{Yuugi Punishment}

================================================
Drink Sake
================================================

Drink Sake is available at 1 star of {Yuugi} {Faith}.
It costs one potion and 1 faith, changing the potion into sake.

================================================
German Suplex
================================================

German Suplex is available at 2 stars of {Yuugi} {Faith}.
It costs 10-15 {Power} and 4 faith and requires an open tile behind you.
Maximum damage is (one-turn melee damage)x(1 + Faith/100) + 2d(2 + Faith/20). Fixed enemies are
immune.
A {Caught} target can be suplexed without spending faith.

================================================
Throw
================================================

Throw is available at 3 stars of {Yuugi} {Faith}.
It costs 12-20 {Power} and 5 faith. The thrown target takes 5 + its level + Faith/10 damage,
then causes a 3x3 explosion dealing 3d(3 + Faith/15).
A {Caught} target can be thrown without spending faith.

================================================
Howl of Annihilation
================================================

Howl of Annihilation is available at 4 stars of {Yuugi} {Faith}.
It costs 20-30 {Power} and 5 faith and inflicts {Silence} on all visible enemies for (20 +
Faith/5) to (40 + Faith/5) turns.
Targets may resist it, but a {Caught} target cannot.

================================================
Triple Ultimate Technique
================================================

Triple Ultimate Technique is available at 5 stars of {Yuugi} {Faith}.
It costs 100 {Power} and 10 faith, producing one explosion per turn for 3 turns. Their radii
are 2, 3, and 4, and each deals 4d(4 + stage + Faith/20) damage.

================================================
Yuugi Punishment
================================================

Under {Yuugi} {Punishment}, one effect is chosen:
25%: become confused for 9-30 turns and {Drunk} for 50-100 turns.
25%: a giant swing teleports you to a random adjacent tile and removes 30-60% of current {HP}.
50%: summon (1 + {Level}/10) to (1 + Level/5) hostile oni for 40-60 turns.

================================================
Junko
================================================

Junko is a purified divine spirit and a {God} suited to characters who fight with {Danmaku} and
buffs.
Only your own kills grant {Faith}: 18% for weak enemies and 100% for strong enemies.
Her powers are strong, but using them temporarily applies {Purification}, restricting spell
cards, scrolls, and potions.
Abandoning Junko gives 30 {Punishment}.
-> {Simple Danmaku}, {Pure Strength}, {Pristine Murderous Intent}, {Blessing of Purification},
{Junko Punishment}

Purification
================================================

Purification is a penalty caused by {Junko}'s powers and has three stages.
Stage 1 prevents using {Spell Cards}. Stage 2 also prevents most {Scrolls}, except Identify,
Enchant, Remove Curse, and Acquirement. Stage 3 also prevents potion effects.
Its duration is shown separately for each stage.

================================================
Simple Danmaku
================================================

Simple Danmaku is available at 1 star of {Junko} {Faith}.
It costs 1 {MP} and 5-10 {Power}, firing a projectile with 3d(4 + Faith/10) damage and 10 +
Faith/15 accuracy.
It adds 0.5 stage of {Purification} for 20-30 turns.

================================================
Pure Strength
================================================

Pure Strength is available at 2 stars of {Junko} {Faith}.
It costs 3 {MP} and 40-60 {Power}, granting {Strength Increase} for 25 + Power/5 + 1d(Faith/2)
turns.
It adds 1 stage of {Purification} for the same duration.

================================================
Pristine Murderous Intent
================================================

Pristine Murderous Intent is available at 4 stars of {Junko} {Faith}.
It costs 5 {MP} and 70-100 {Power}, granting {Murderous Intent} for 25 + Faith/5 + 1d(Faith/2)
turns.
It adds 2 stages of {Purification} for the same duration.

================================================
Blessing of Purification
================================================

Blessing of Purification is a once-per-game power available at 6 stars of {Junko} {Faith}.
Choose one permanent blessing: Skill, Resistance, Mana, Power, Life, Equipment, or System.
Skill makes one chosen skill level always equal your character {Level}.
Resistance grants immunity to one chosen element among fire, cold, and electricity.
Mana makes {HP} and {MP} function as a single resource.
Power doubles maximum {Power} and Power recovery.
Life grants two extra lives.
Equipment raises one chosen item to its maximum enchantment +5, including artifacts.
System allows {Spell Cards} during {Purification} and triples talisman recharge speed.
Refusing the blessing lowers faith to 140.

Junko Punishment
================================================

{Junko} always applies {Purification} for 50-80 turns under {Punishment}, then chooses one
effect:
33%: {Weakened} for 50-80 turns. 33%: {Slow} for 50-80 turns. 33%: lower a random stat by 1-2
three times.

Shizuha (Shizuha Aki)

================================================
Shizuha Punishment
