Wizard Mode can be activated with & when the game is launched using the --wizard option.
(It is designed for development convenience rather than actual gameplay.
   It may contain many bugs and be difficult to use.)

§yA§d      - Generate Item (Beta)
§yC§d      - Clear Current Floor & Merge Items
§yD§d      - Magic Mapping
§yE§d      - Auto Explore
§yG§d      - Move to Another Floor
§yH§d      - Restore HP/MP/P
§yI§d      - Identify All Items
§yb§d      - Blink
§ym§d      - Spawn Monster
§yp§d      - Create Altar
§yq§d      - Print Required EXP
§yR§d      - Rebuild Current Floor
§yw§d      - Trigger Weather
§yW§d      - Stop Time
§yX§d      - Level Up Once
§y>§d      - Go Down a Floor
§y<§d      - Go Up a Floor
§y^§d      - Increase Faith by 10
