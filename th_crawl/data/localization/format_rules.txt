Touhou Crawl localization format rules

All localization files use UTF-8 text.
The following tags are processed only when the string is passed through
LocalzationManager::formatString or LocalzationManager::printLogWithKey.

Common syntax

{0}
    Replaced with placeholder 0. Placeholder indexes start at 0.

{0:option1|option2}
    Applies a language helper to placeholder 0.
    The available helpers are listed in each language section below.

{0==text?true:false}
{0!=text?true:false}
    Selects text according to a case-insensitive comparison with placeholder 0.
    This conditional syntax is supported by printLogWithKey only.
    It is not processed by formatString.
    Nested braces and colons cannot be used inside either result.

{choice1|choice2|choice3}
    Randomly selects one choice whenever the string is processed.
    A random-choice tag cannot begin with a number.

\n
    Inserts a line break. Write it as the two characters backslash and n.


[KOR] 한국어

조사 자동 선택

{0:은|는}  {0:이|가}  {0:을|를}  {0:과|와}
{0:으로|로}  {0:이라|라}  {0:이다|다}  {0:이고|고}

받침 유무에 따라 알맞은 조사를 붙인다. 순서를 뒤집어 적어도 동작한다.
"으로|로"는 ㄹ 받침 예외도 처리한다.

예시:
    {0:은|는} 문을 열었다.
    {0:이|가} {1:을|를} 공격했다.
    {0:으로|로} 변했다.

주의:
    값의 마지막 글자가 완성형 한글이 아니면 첫 번째 조사를 사용한다.
    조사까지 문장에 따로 적지 말고 반드시 플레이스홀더 안에 넣는다.
    조사 선택은 단수와 복수를 판단하지 않는다.


[ENG] English

Player and third-person agreement

The player's localized name is "You". Conditional comparison is
case-insensitive, so compare it with "you".

Examples:
    {0} hit{0==you?:s} {1}.
        You hit the target.
        The fairy hits the target.

    {0} miss{0==you?:es} {1}.
        You miss the target.
        The fairy misses the target.

    {0} block{0!=you?s:} {1}.
        You block it.
        The fairy blocks it.

    {0} {0==you?are:is} confused.
        You are confused.
        The fairy is confused.

Possessives

    {0}{0==you?r:'s} weapon
        your weapon
        the fairy's weapon

Articles

    {0:a|an}
        Places "a" or "an" before placeholder 0.
        The choice checks only the first English letter, not its pronunciation.

    {1!=you?the :}{1}
        Adds "the" for a non-player target.

Plural helper

    {0:is|are}
        Chooses "is" or "are" from the plural flag supplied by
        PlaceHolderHelper in the code. This does not detect plurality from text.
        Do not use it to distinguish "You" from a third-person subject;
        use {0==you?are:is} for that case.

Notes:
    Conditional branches may be empty, as in {0==you?:s}.
    Use printLogWithKey for strings containing == or != conditionals.
    formatString currently leaves those conditionals unchanged.
